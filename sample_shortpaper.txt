Methodology
   The development of StuOrganize followed a hybrid
Waterfall-Agile methodology. Waterfall was used during
early requirement and design phases because these phases
required stable documentation, diagrams, and approval before
implementation. Agile was then used during implementation
and testing, where the system was developed through feature-
based iterations.
